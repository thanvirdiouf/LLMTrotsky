# Reviewed compact factual forms

The blinded second review found a real rendering limit: a request for one
sentence receives a two-sentence immutable card, and source boilerplate can
crowd out a useful judgment. Cutting a qualified statement after a word limit
would risk changing its certainty. V18 instead tests separately reviewed
compact canonical formulations.

Four optional forms cover the radio prediction and three ILO claims. A separate
AI source reviewer accepted two proposals and revised two. The revisions restore
the ILO upper-threshold condition and date the prospective transformation
assessment. The continuing coder accepted those revisions against the original
packet context. This is AI-assisted source curation, not human historian
validation. Original statements, source blocks, anchors, speaker roles, dates,
statuses, ten packets and 39 proposition IDs remain unchanged.

The experimental source plan is `config/ghost_source_plan_v3.json`; private
historical/contemporary source specifications carry the forms and their review
provenance. Each form is bound to its text fingerprint and exact source-packet
fingerprint. Changed text or context requires new review. The immutable full
claim remains available. Exact quotation rendering always uses original anchors,
never the paraphrased compact form.

Brief or explicitly short factual replies prefer an approved form when one
exists. The packer counts complete forms against sentence/word targets rather
than cutting them. Two short ILO forms can preserve the dated exposure estimate
and full-implementation condition within the opening; a single form can satisfy
a one-sentence limit. Cards without a compact form retain their original text.
Ordinary full-detail source statements stay full. Selection receives the same
original propositions; the added forms do not become invented evidence IDs.

The factual segment records its form. Validation computes text from the reviewed
card, ignoring model-supplied replacements. Final diagnostics count actual
sentences and words as well as targets. Those counts are conservative estimates,
not a claim of linguistic perfection or semantic completeness. Transient form
choices stay out of verified memory. Hash checks establish integrity, not truth.

## V18 controlled trial

The trial uses the same reviewer-authored questions, profile, source collection,
evaluator and base settings as V16. Full original proposition fields are
identical; only four optional formulations/review provenance are added. This is
a combined application/data intervention, not a component ablation. The active
pointer remains on v2 until the trial is reviewed; `build_ghost_smoke.py
--source-plan config/ghost_source_plan_v3.json` freezes the alternate evidence
without changing that pointer.

The observed singular/plural `premise_correction(s)` spellings normalize to the
same factual task, preserving the model's selected evidence. Other unknown
modes remain blocked. The broad finite-selection experiment stays opt-in and
is disabled here: its valid output format caused two previously useful tasks
to become unnecessary refusals in blinded review. This trial preserves the
baseline unconstrained selection behavior while recovering the unambiguous
factual label.

Primary checks: recover the answerable historical-summary case, retain
appropriate refusal for the absent event, preserve the opening's date/quantity/
full-implementation limits, and produce an actual one-sentence limit. Also
inspect closing word counts, question completeness, unsupported contractual
assurances and argumentative depth. Valid source cards and compact wording are
not sufficient if the actual reply misses the current request.

Sixty focused tests pass. The full suite runs 333 tests with the same two
pre-existing historical README snapshot errors. No locks are rewritten. Each
Kaggle run remains ten replies/at most thirty calls plus one memory probe;
no training, adapters or paid compute. Both adapters remain experimental.
