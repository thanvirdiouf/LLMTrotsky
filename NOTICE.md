## Historical sources and datasets

The project uses historical texts hosted by the
[Marxists Internet Archive](https://www.marxists.org/), including its
[Leon Trotsky archive](https://www.marxists.org/archive/trotsky/).
Credit the archive and the individual work's URL when reusing its material.
Translations, annotations, transcriptions, and editorial additions may have
rights distinct from the underlying historical work. Public-domain status
depends on the work and jurisdiction.

MIA's [terms of use](https://www.marxists.org/admin/legal/terms-of-use.htm)
require users to check the status of individual documents. MIA cannot grant
republication permission for all third-party copyrighted works it hosts.
Volunteer-created material is covered by the Creative Commons terms identified
by MIA; retain the applicable attribution and license notices.

The supplied corpus, downloaded HTML, extracted texts, training examples,
source-based review records, evaluation passages, and generated notebooks
containing these materials are excluded from this Git repository. They remain
local artifacts. Their exclusion is not a declaration that they are all
restricted: publication requires a separate review of their provenance,
permissions, and applicable terms. Historical quotations, work titles, and
third-party references appearing in code or documentation retain their own
status and are not relicensed by the software license.

## Dependencies and model artifacts

Python packages are installed separately and retain their respective licenses.
The software license does not replace dependency notices or license conditions.
Model weights, tokenizers, adapters, checkpoints, and downloaded run bundles
are not distributed in this repository. Their redistribution requires checking
the selected model revision's terms as well as the training material's rights.
